Statement of Faith

▼

The Holy Bible is the inspired Word of God and the foundation of this statement. This church adheres to the Baptist Faith and Message adopted by the Southern Baptist Convention in 1963 (as revised in 2000) (中文, English). We are a body of believers, united by baptism in Christ Jesus, committed to sharing the good news of salvation with the lost. The ordinances observed by this church are Baptism and the Lord's Supper.

Church Functions

▼

Worship is the Christian way of life, Fellowship is the connection among members in the Lord, Discipleship training helps us learn to be more like Jesus, Church service allows us to grow in ministry, and Missions is the Great Commission entrusted to us by Christ. These five functions stem from the life change that follows accepting salvation.

Brief Church History

▼

Under God's guidance, this church was established in January 1981 by Pastor Jianzhong Zhou, who gathered over a dozen brothers and sisters. Initially, the congregation met at the **Oakland Mill Village Center**, and in September of the same year, moved to the **Wilde Lake Village Center**. In August 1984, the church relocated to the **Owen Brown Village Center**. With assistance from the Howard County Baptist Association, the church moved into **Ellicott City Bethel Baptist Church** in June 1995. In November 2001, we purchased the former six-acre Lions Club site, which became our permanent church building.
Through the years, God's grace has been with us! God's great power has continually led us! Several faithful servants of God have labored diligently to serve this church, pastoring many brothers and sisters. The church has grown from a few early families to a large family in the Lord with nearly a hundred families today. The original Mandarin Chinese worship service has developed into simultaneous Mandarin and English worship services.
While building the spiritual lives of our brothers and sisters through the various church functions, we also expand God's kingdom by participating in mission work in Mainland China and Taiwan.
